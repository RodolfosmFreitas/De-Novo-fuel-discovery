# Abstract

The aviation industry’s dependence on liquid fossil fuels makes it one of the most difficult sectors to reach net-zero emissions. Sustainable aviation fuels (SAF) offer a promising solution; however, their implementation is hampered by high production costs and the lack of systematic design tools to produce 100\% drop-in fuels. This work introduces an AI-guided de novo fuel discovery framework that employs deep kernel learning to establish a high-fidelity compositional mapping between chemical structures and key physicochemical properties of fuels. The AI-driven compositional mapping achieves exceptional predictive accuracy, with a coefficient of determination exceeding 0.91 across all properties, and offers calibrated uncertainty estimates, which are crucial for predictive accuracy aligned with regulatory standards. Integrated with a virtual high-throughput screening platform, the compositional mapping facilitated the prompt identification of SAF blends that comply with or exceed the performance of JP-8 and Jet A reference fuels, while achieving a reduction in particulate emissions of over 17%. The designed fuels fulfil certification standards and are compatible with current aircraft gauging systems. These results establish a scalable, uncertainty-aware framework that accelerates SAF innovation and supports the aviation sector’s transition to net-zero emissions.
