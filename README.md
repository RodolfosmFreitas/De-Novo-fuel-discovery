# An AI-driven framework for the de novo design and optimisation of sustainable aviation fuels

![figure1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4c06f787-dbcc-4920-b4b9-3343715e94dd)



# Abstract

The aviation industry’s dependence on liquid fossil fuels makes it one of the most difficult sectors to reach net-zero emissions. Sustainable aviation fuels (SAF) offer a promising solution; however, their implementation is hampered by high production costs and the lack of systematic design tools to produce 100% drop-in fuels. Considering the substantial potential in enhancing SAF production and utilisation, this work establishes an AI-guided de novo fuel discovery framework that employs deep kernel learning to develop a high-fidelity compositional mapping between chemical structures and key physicochemical properties of fuels. The AI-driven compositional mapping achieves exceptional predictive accuracy, with a coefficient of determination exceeding 0.91 across all properties, and offers calibrated uncertainty estimates, which are crucial for predictive accuracy aligned with regulatory standards. Integrated with a virtual high-throughput screening platform, the compositional mapping facilitated the prompt identification of SAF blends that comply with or exceed the performance of JP-8 and Jet A reference fuels, while achieving a reduction in particulate emissions of over 17%. The formulated fuels fulfil certification standards and are compatible with current aircraft gauging systems. These results represent a significant step forward in the rational formulation of sustainable fuels, establishing an AI-enabled framework that combines predictive accuracy, uncertainty-aware, and virtual high-throughput screening to improve production, reduce costs, and accelerate SAF innovation, thereby supporting the aviation sector’s transition to net-zero emissions.
